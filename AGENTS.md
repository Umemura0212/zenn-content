# Repository instructions

When creating or editing a Zenn article, follow [the canonical Zenn writing and quality skill](.agents/skills/zenn-technical-article/SKILL.md). For final readability review, use [the short editorial checklist](.agents/skills/zenn-overview-writing/SKILL.md).

- Answer one reader question (or one clearly defined relationship between concepts) per article.
- Start with the reader's problem and a verified primary source. State what the reader will be able to understand or decide.
- Distinguish official product behavior, design proposals, and untested effects. Do not invent evidence, novelty, or evaluation results.
- Include a useful example, comparison, or failure case, not an exhaustive how-to.
- Check technical links, feature tiers, version constraints, diagrams, numbers, and consistency across related articles.
- Never disclose confidential information; public Draft PRs are publicly accessible.
- Keep all drafts `published: false` and do not merge or publish without the owner's explicit approval.
- No additional paid APIs, service charges, or permission expansions without prior approval.
