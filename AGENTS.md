# Repository instructions

When creating or editing a Zenn article, read and follow [the Zenn technical article skill](.agents/skills/zenn-technical-article/SKILL.md).

- Keep each article focused on one standalone technical concept.
- Cite verifiable primary sources, distinguish historical references from newly researched ones.
- Never disclose confidential information.
- Keep all drafts `published: false`; do not merge or publish without the owner's explicit approval.
- No additional paid APIs, service charges, or permission expansions without prior approval.
